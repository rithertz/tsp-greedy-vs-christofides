Observations:
  - Christofides is designed for metric TSP and should stay close to the 1.5x guarantee.
  - Greedy is faster but can occasionally exceed the 1.5× bound.
  - For small n, both algorithms are near-optimal; differences grow with n.
  - Clustered instances sometimes yield shorter tours than random ones.
